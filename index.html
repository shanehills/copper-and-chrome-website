Our Story

Nano-Brewery.
Maximum Flavour.

We're a small-batch nano-brewery tucked away in West Berkshire, driven by a simple belief: that great beer doesn't need a factory. Every brew is crafted by hand, in small quantities, with an obsessive attention to flavour.

From our traditional house bitter to our bold hoppy ales, every pint that leaves our brewery carries the character of something made with genuine care — and a healthy disregard for the ordinary.
